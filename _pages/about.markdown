# About Owen McQuarrie

I develop and support for entire software lifecycles. I have experience with design, project management, full stack development, release management/pipelines, test automation, and production support.

My current professional tech stack consists of ... Tosca for test automation, and SonarQube + Sentry for acceptance and logging. 

I believe that appropriately addressing the insane velocity of AI-assited development is the future of software engineering. As engineers, building a full suite of skills - including those typically reserved for other business roles - is key to orchestrating increasingly automated SDLCs.
